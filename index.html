Privacy Policy for MERCURY Business Manager

Effective date: September 19, 2026 · Last updated: September 19, 2026

MERCURY Business Manager ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is developed and operated by YusrTec ("Company"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application MERCURY Business Manager (package name: com.yusrtec.mercury.businessmanager).

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to minimizing data collection. We only collect information necessary to provide and improve the App.

1.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, and business details if you create an account.
  • Business data: Client records, invoices, inventory, notes, and documents you enter.
  • Support communications: Your name, email, and message content when you contact us.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: Device model, OS version, app version, language, and region.
  • Usage data: Feature usage, session duration, and interaction events (only if analytics is enabled).
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Crash logs and performance data (only if crash reporting is enabled).

1.3 Permissions We Request

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to capture photos or scan documents. Images are processed and stored locally on your device.
  • Storage / Files: Used to let you select, save, and share files through the Android system file picker.
  • Internet: Used to sync data, load content, and send crash reports if enabled.
  • Notifications: Used to send reminders and updates you have opted into.

We do not access your contacts, call logs, SMS, precise location, or microphone unless you explicitly grant permission for a specific feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • Enable core features such as client management, invoicing, and document capture.
  • Improve performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience.
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Send important notices, updates, and security alerts.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

  • Service providers: Google Play Services, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, and cloud hosting providers.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.
  • With your consent: For any other purpose with your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage: Most business data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you enable cloud sync.
  • Encryption: We use HTTPS/TLS for data in transit and secure storage on device.
  • Access controls: Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel.
  • No method is 100% secure: We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@yusrtec.com.
